PRIVACY

A few things to know about your visit.

THE ESSENTIALS

Yourfashioncurator is an independent fashion journal. We do not offer public reader accounts. If you choose to subscribe to THE YFC LETTER, we collect your email address to send a weekly roundup of published articles on Sundays at 18:00 Europe/Zurich. Subscriptions require your consent and email confirmation, and you can unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter. Technical data, such as IP addresses and browser requests, may be processed to operate and protect the site.

OUR SERVICES

Cloudflare Pages hosts the site; Supabase stores articles, images and private editor accounts. Brevo will manage newsletter subscriptions, subscription confirmation and email delivery when the newsletter is activated. We also use Google Fonts and jsDelivr. These services may process technical information when you visit.

ADS & EXTERNAL LINKS

Google AdSense code is installed while advertising approval is pending. If ads are enabled, cookies or similar technologies may be used, subject to applicable consent requirements. Some articles may link to external sites or, in future, contain clearly disclosed affiliate links.

CONTACT

For privacy questions or requests about your data, contact us through the email listed in the ABOUT ME section of the homepage.
