THE JOURNAL / SITE INFORMATION

PRIVACY

A short guide to how this independent fashion journal operates.

WHO RUNS THIS SITE?

Yourfashioncurator is an independent fashion journal. To contact the publisher about privacy or content, use the email link in the ABOUT ME section of the homepage.

WHAT INFORMATION IS PROCESSED?

When you visit the site, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and request logs to deliver and secure the pages. The public site does not currently offer visitor account registration or a newsletter form.

The editor's private login uses Supabase Authentication. Editorial posts and uploaded media are stored through Supabase. These admin features are not meant for public visitor accounts.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The site is hosted with Netlify. It connects to Supabase to retrieve published articles and site information. It loads web fonts from Google Fonts and uses a JavaScript library delivered through jsDelivr.

The site also includes a Google AdSense identification script while advertising approval is being assessed. If advertising is enabled in the future, Google and advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies, subject to applicable consent requirements and choices. Any necessary advertising consent controls must be configured before serving personalized ads.

EXTERNAL LINKS AND AFFILIATION

Some posts may link to independent retailers or social media platforms. Once affiliate links are introduced, eligible purchases made through those links may generate a commission for the magazine. Such links and sponsored content should be clearly identified where used. External sites have their own privacy practices.

YOUR QUESTIONS

For information about personal data or requests concerning your data, contact the publisher using the email address displayed on the homepage. We will review requests in line with applicable law.
